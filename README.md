# 📚 tsundoku-psp - Read Manga Anywhere on Your PSP

## 🎮 What Is This?

tsundoku-psp is a simple manga reader designed specifically for the PlayStation Portable (PSP). If you love reading comics or manga and own a PSP, this app lets you turn your handheld console into a portable library. Instead of carrying heavy books or squinting at your phone, you can enjoy your favorite stories right on your PSP screen.

The name comes from the Japanese word "tsundoku" (積ん読), which means buying books and letting them pile up unread. This app helps you finally read all those digital manga files you've collected!

## ✨ Key Features

- **CBZ File Support** – Reads CBZ files, which are the standard format for digital comics and manga
- **PSP Optimized** – Designed to work smoothly on PSP hardware
- **Easy Navigation** – Simple controls to flip pages and jump between chapters
- **Portable Library** – Store hundreds of manga volumes on your PSP memory card
- **Free and Open Source** – No cost, no ads, no hidden fees

### Step 2: Prepare Your PSP

Before you can use tsundoku-psp, you need to make sure your PSP is ready. Here's what you need:

- A PSP console (any model works: PSP 1000, 2000, 3000, or PSP Go)
- A Memory Stick Duo or Memory Stick Micro with at least 100MB of free space
- Your PSP should be running custom firmware (CFW). If you're not sure what this means, search online for "PSP custom firmware guide" – it's a common process that many PSP owners do to unlock extra features.

### Step 3: Transfer the Files

Once you've downloaded the application:

1. Connect your PSP to your computer using a USB cable
2. Your PSP should appear as a removable drive on your computer
3. Open the drive and look for a folder called "PSP" (if you don't see it, create one)
4. Inside the "PSP" folder, look for or create a folder called "GAME"
5. Copy the downloaded tsundoku-psp folder into the "GAME" folder
6. Safely eject your PSP from your computer

### Step 4: Add Your Manga

Now you need to get your manga files onto your PSP:

1. Create a folder on your PSP called "Manga" (or any name you like)
2. Copy your CBZ files into this folder
3. Make sure your CBZ files are named clearly, like "OnePiece_Vol01.cbz" – this makes them easier to find in the app

Don't have CBZ files? You can download free manga from public domain sources or convert your own digital comics using free tools like Calibre or ComicRack.

### Step 5: Launch the App

1. Turn on your PSP
2. Go to the Game menu on your PSP home screen
3. Look for "tsundoku-psp" in the Memory Stick section
4. Press X to launch it

That's it! You should now see the tsundoku-psp interface on your PSP screen.

## 🛠️ Troubleshooting

If something isn't working, try these common fixes:

**App doesn't appear on my PSP**
- Double-check that you placed the folder in the correct location (PSP/GAME)
- Make sure your PSP has custom firmware installed
- Try restarting your PSP

**CBZ files won't open**
- Verify your files are actually CBZ format (they should end in .cbz)
- Try re-downloading or converting the files
- Check that the files aren't corrupted

**App crashes or freezes**
- Make sure you have enough free space on your memory card
- Try deleting and re-copying the app files
- Check if there's a newer version available
